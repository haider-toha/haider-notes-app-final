Gear-Closed Twin-Crank Loop with Pendulum Trains

An original four‑degree‑of‑freedom, energy‑conserving multibody system

Generalized coordinates  q = (θ1, φa, φb, φc) ·  an external spur‑gear mesh closes a kinematic loop and counter‑couples the two cranks (θ2 = −(R1/R2) θ1) ·  a double pendulum rides crank 1, a single pendulum rides crank 2 ·  no driving force, no damping

Two cranks turn on fixed, parallel axles O1 and O2. Rigidly keyed to each crank is a spur gear — pitch radius R1 at O1, R2 at O2 — and the two gears mesh externally (centre distance R1+R2). Their pitch circles roll without slip, so equal, oppositely‑directed pitch‑point arcs give one holonomic relation R1 θ1 = −R2 θ2 that closes the loop and slaves crank 2 to counter‑rotate. Off crank 1's tip hangs a double pendulum (rods a, b at absolute angles φa, φb); off crank 2's tip hangs a single pendulum (rod c at φc). Only gravity loads the system and the gear mesh is ideal (it stores no energy), so there is no actuator, no spring, no damper and total mechanical energy E = T + V is exactly conserved. Because both cranks are pinned to ground, no linear or angular momentum survives — energy is the sole invariant.

E is computed directly from the Cartesian kinetic + potential energy of the five rigid rods (mass‑centre velocities from the analytic position Jacobian, plus each rod's spin) — independently of the M, F that drive the motion — so a flat E is a genuine check on the equations, not a tautology. The gear readout R1θ1+R2θ2 holds at 0 (mesh closure).

Method. The model was derived from scratch with the Lagrangian recipe surveyed from the Learn Multibody Dynamics reference scripts (Moore, CC BY 4.0): place the five rods by absolute angles about their fixed pivots, write the external‑mesh relation R1θ1 = −R2θ2 and fold it into the coordinate choice (minimal‑coordinate loop closure), assemble the kinetic and potential energy, apply the Euler–Lagrange operator in the four independent coordinates, reduce to the canonical positive‑definite M(q) q̈ = F(q,q̇) first‑order form, integrate with fixed‑step RK4, and verify energy conservation by recomputing total mechanical energy from the state at every step (max relative drift ≈ 2.8×10−7 over 40 s at dt = 0.001 s, falling as O(dt4)). The system itself — a gear‑closed twin‑crank loop carrying a double and a single pendulum train — is an original construction, not reproduced from any worked example.
