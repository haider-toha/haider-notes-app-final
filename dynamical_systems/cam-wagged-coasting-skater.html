Cam-Wagged Coasting Skater

An original four‑degree‑of‑freedom, energy‑conserving, free‑floating multibody system

Generalized coordinates  q = (x, y, θ, ψ) ·  a knife‑edge blade (nonholonomic rolling‑without‑slip) under a free chassis ·  a spinning cam wags a pinned follower through the groove law β = β0 + A sin ψ ·  gravity out of plane, no spring, no driving force, no damping

A rigid chassis coasts on a frictionless horizontal ice sheet, carried on a single knife‑edge blade fixed a distance a ahead of its mass‑centre. The blade may slide along the heading but never across it — a genuine nonholonomic rolling‑without‑slip constraint. Mounted at the chassis centre is a uniform cam disc; its rotation ψ (relative to the chassis) drives a pinned follower rod through an ideal cam‑follower groove, slaving the follower‑to‑chassis angle to β = β0 + A sin ψ. Because gravity acts out of the plane (it is carried by the ice’s normal force and does no in‑plane work) and there is no spring, no actuator, no damper, the potential energy is identically zero and total mechanical energy E = T is exactly conserved. As the cam wags the follower, the workless knife‑edge silently trades the follower’s inertial reaction into forward surge and yaw: the skater self‑propels while coasting, a reaction‑wheel‑style drift. The knife‑edge exerts a lateral impulse, so neither linear nor angular momentum is conserved — energy is the sole invariant.

E = T is computed directly from the Cartesian kinetic energy of the three rigid bodies (mass‑centre velocities from the knife‑edge speed recovery, plus each body’s spin) — independently of the M, F that drive the motion — so a flat E is a genuine check, not a tautology. The check has teeth precisely because the per‑body energies swing wildly (the cam pumps its spin energy into forward translation, a reaction‑wheel coast) while their sum stays pinned. The knife‑edge readout vC·ê2 holds at 0 (no lateral slip).

Method. The model was derived from scratch with the Lagrangian recipe surveyed from the Learn Multibody Dynamics reference scripts (Moore, CC BY 4.0): place the three bodies with a free chassis mass‑centre (x, y), yaw θ, and relative cam angle ψ; fold the two pins and the cam‑follower groove β = β0 + A sin ψ into the parameterization; write the knife‑edge rolling constraint −ẋ sinθ + ẏ cosθ + a θ̇ = 0 and confirm it is nonholonomic (cross‑partial exactness test); assemble the kinetic energy (V = 0, gravity out of plane); apply the Euler–Lagrange operator and project onto the admissible directions (Lagrange–d’Alembert), reducing to the canonical positive‑definite M(ψ) [v̇, θ̈, ψ̈]T = F; integrate the full pose with fixed‑step RK4 through the speed‑recovery relations; and verify conservation by recomputing total mechanical energy from the physical per‑body kinematics at every step (max relative drift ≈ 3.4×10−9 over 60 s at dt = 0.001 s, falling as O(dt4)). The system itself — a free‑floating knife‑edge skater self‑propelled only by a cam‑wagged follower — is an original construction, not reproduced from any worked example.
