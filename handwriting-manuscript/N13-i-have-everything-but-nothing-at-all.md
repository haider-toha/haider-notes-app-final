i have everything but nothing at all

i'm twenty-three. if you saw my life written
down you'd probably think i had it figured
out. good grades in school, then imperial, goldman
after that, the job everyone tells you to
chase and i lasted one quarter before i
left because i couldn't make myself care about
it. then a yc startup, founding engineer. five
countries in the last eight months. i play
a lot of sport. my parents are good
to me and i mean that and i
have friends who'd pick up if i called
them in the middle of the night. and
i pray five times a day, i keep
my fasts, i try to do everything i'm
meant to do as a muslim and most
days i manage it.

and i still feel empty a lot of
the time. that's the thing i don't really
know how to say to anyone, because it
sounds ungrateful and it probably is.
