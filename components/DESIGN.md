# Lined notebook

The notebook is the main site. `/`, `/contents`, and `/notebook` always land on the two preliminary contents leaves, even when a reading position is saved. `/all`, existing folder URLs, and published note URLs open reading pages.

The open notebook is the entire visible interface. White desk, ivory ruled paper, a narrow binding, stacked paper edges, blue-black ink (#303f53), and understated blue links (#355b78). No toolbar, footer, font picker, corner labels, decorative corner folds, or hover animation.

Reenie Beanie supplies the handwriting. Notebook Math is a matching OFL derivative that adds missing Greek and mathematical symbols; both fonts and their licenses are served locally. Body text uses 24px type on a 29px ruling (21px on compact desktop, 23px on mobile); the opening title uses 62px (48px on mobile). Page numbers always use 12px system sans-serif with lining, tabular numerals.

Desktop shows two 720px-tall pages in a spread up to 1080px wide. Narrow layouts show one 1000px-tall page. Keep the responsive engine minimum-width override: removing it causes horizontal overflow. Long passages scroll within the ruled writing area.

Deliberate outer-edge dragging/tapping turns a soft sheet across the spine. StPageFlip 2.0.7 supplies the fold geometry, reverse face, and moving shadows (950ms release animation, 0.28 maximum shadow opacity). The lifecycle adapter cancels the renderer on unmount. Hover keeps the page flat. Reduced motion changes pages immediately.

Invisible 24px-wide full-height outer-edge controls retain accessible labels and keyboard focus. Arrow keys turn pages when the spread has focus; they never intercept links. Hidden pages are inert. Page position remains available to screen readers. Content is authored in `constants.tsx`; there are no scratchpads or browser-local drafts. External links open in a new tab.

Reading pages combine consecutive paragraphs, with blank lines between them. Keep the opening identity page; balance the final passages to avoid a nearly empty final reading page. Preserve the 24px handwriting and 29px rule rhythm rather than shrinking type. Side grabs map pointer displacement from an outer-boundary anchor, preventing an oversized fold at the first movement.

A removable sheet uses outward rubber-band resistance followed by a damped spring release. The implementation uses unit mass, translational stiffness 240/damping 29, angular stiffness 115/damping 16, bounded frame time, and velocity-dependent torque around the grab position. Reduced motion removes the spring delay and unfurl effect. Heading alignment, padding, background and scroll position are captured from the source rather than recomposed. Reveal real underlying-page content during removal. The sheet returns by aligning its binding edge to the spine, with no extra recovery control.

Opening reading page: a 62px Reenie Beanie name (48px on mobile), location immediately below on a 29px line, then social links after one blank ruled line. After a two-rule gap below the social links, place the original opening paragraph about current work. Maintain generous margins; move subsequent paragraphs to the following pages without repetition. No running-heading label, repeated name, tagline, or contents list on the identity leaf. Preserve the empty running-head element's geometry because detached-sheet presentation reads that element.

Paper details stay still at rest. A completed turn gets a 310ms flex of less than one degree on the inner article; the renderer retains sole ownership of leaf transforms. Cut edges appear along the bottom and outer sides. Read and unread stacks range from 1–13px with reading progress, with final-spread alignment on desktop. Reverse ink is mirrored from the opposite face, aria-hidden, noninteractive and faint (about 2% at rest, 5–6% while lifting). No effect introduces a separate layer that captures grabs.

Contents occupies two actual preliminary leaves in the same page-turn engine, numbered i and ii. Show “contents” only on the left leaf; an empty 58px spacer on the right aligns both columns. Separate groups by one 29px ruled line, omit dotted leaders, and write reference page numbers in the same 22px handwriting. The contents leaves keep their 29px running-head row for the navigation control and use a 9px gap before the contents ink, which fits the remaining writing area. A small handwritten control inside each page’s upper outer margin reads “← contents” and navigates to `/contents` during reading; on the preliminary leaves, “continue reading →” restores the remembered page and its canonical note URL. Each control belongs to its sheet and turns with the paper, including the reverse face; never position one shared control over the spread. Desktop controls mirror into the outer corners; mobile keeps the control at the upper left. The control shares the running-head row: reserve room beside long note headings and retain a 44px touch target on mobile. Contents has a real route and browser-history entry, and remains the default landing surface. No ribbon, protruding tab, popup or separate contents surface remains. Reading pages retain numbering from 1; route and storage indices exclude the two preliminary leaves. Store the remembered reading position as a note ID and source offset, preserving it through repagination and visits to contents.

Expanded media uses a paper-colored accessible dialog with pinned ink controls. Close, zoom and reset stay outside the transformed drawing; keyboard focus stays inside the dialog and returns to the opener. Background pages are inert while the viewer is open.

The notebook includes all source notes through `notebookPages.ts`. Pagination preserves every source character and keeps code fences, diagrams, simulations, equations and tables intact.
